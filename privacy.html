🛡️ Our Core Data Promise: Zero Financial Data Storage & Zero Sale to Tipsters

Dalal Street Alpha does NOT collect, request, or store sensitive financial data such as bank account credentials, PAN numbers, trading PINs, demat holdings, or transaction records. We maintain an absolute zero-tolerance policy against selling user data to third-party telemarketers, advisory boiler rooms, or stock tip services.

1. Information We Collect Automatically

When you access our web portal, our hosting infrastructure (Vercel Edge Network) automatically records standard web traffic telemetry:

  • Device & Browser Data: Operating system, browser type, screen resolution, and language settings.
  • Server Logs: IP address (anonymized where possible), referral URLs, request timestamps, and pages visited.
  • Performance Telemetry: Core Web Vitals (LCP, CLS, FID) to ensure low-latency loading of our quantitative charts.

2. Cookies and Local Storage

We use minimal, privacy-respecting cookies and browser local storage:

  • Functional Local Storage: Remembers your preferred parameters in the 24-Month RevShare Simulator and Fee Calculator so you don't have to re-enter them.
  • Third-Party Embeds: When viewing embedded YouTube video broadcasts on our site, YouTube may set cookies in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Outbound Affiliate Attribution: Clicking partner links (e.g., to Dhan, Zerodha, or TradingView) sets a standard partner referral cookie on the destination broker domain to attribute your 10% fee rebate.

3. How We Use Information

Any telemetry gathered is used exclusively to:

  • Maintain website stability, prevent distributed denial-of-service (DDoS) attacks, and secure our quantitative API endpoints.
  • Optimize content performance, article rendering speed, and calculator responsiveness.
  • Comply with applicable statutory requirements under Indian law.

4. Data Retention and Security

We deploy enterprise-grade security protocols, including HTTPS (TLS 1.3 encryption), strict Content Security Policies (CSP), and nosniff HTTP security headers. Log telemetry is retained only for operational diagnostics and is automatically purged on rolling schedules.
