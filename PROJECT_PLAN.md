# Indian Stock Market Content Automation - Project Plan

## 1. SEBI Compliance Framework

### 1.1 Key Distinction: Education vs. Advice

SEBI (Securities and Exchange Board of India) has drawn a clear line between **investor education** and **investment advice/research**. This is the single most important concept for the project.

| Activity | Permitted without SEBI registration? | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| **Investor Education** | YES | Explaining P/E ratio, what is a balance sheet, how FII flows work, general market trends |
| **Investment Advice** | NO | "Buy this stock at ₹1200", "Sell Reliance", your portfolio should have 30% in X |
| **Research Analysis** | NO (needs Research Analyst registration) | "Based on analysis, this stock will reach ₹2000 in 1 year", target prices, recommendations |
| **Data Presentation** | YES | Showing financial metrics, historical data, shareholding patterns without recommendation |
| **News Commentary** | YES | "Company X reported 15% profit growth" - factual reporting without "therefore buy" |

### 1.2 What Anand Srinivasan (Money Pechu) Does

From his Instagram bio: *"Value investor - MoneyPechu: Info is for educational purposes only, We don't endorse products. We aren't SEBI reg, Seek..."*

His approach:
- **NEVER says "Buy" or "Sell"** - Instead says "This is what the data shows"
- **Labels everything as educational** - Clear disclaimer in every post
- **Uses delayed data** - SEBI Jan 2025 circular prohibits unregistered finfluencers from using live stock prices; any stock data shown must be at least 3 months old
- **Teaches concepts, not picks** - Teaches *how* to analyze, not *what* to buy
- **No specific price targets** - Shows historical context, valuation metrics, but no forward-looking price predictions
- **Focus on financial literacy** - All content positions as "understanding finance better"

### 1.3 Project Compliance Rules (MANDATORY)

```
1. DISCLAIMER - Every video (long-form AND short) MUST start with:
   "This content is for educational purposes only. We are not SEBI registered 
    investment advisors. Please consult a certified financial professional 
    before making any investment decisions."

2. NO BUY/SELL/HOLD RECOMMENDATIONS - Never phrase anything as advice.
   Instead use: "The data suggests..." / "Historically..." / "Looking at fundamentals..."

3. "I WOULD" IS SAFE. "YOU SHOULD" IS NOT - Personal opinion framing is permitted:
   ✅ "I would buy this stock if it drops to ₹2500" (personal opinion)
   ✅ "I would hold until the next earnings report" (personal strategy)
   ❌ "You should buy at ₹2500" (recommendation/advice)
   ❌ "Buy now, target ₹3000" (stock tip)
   The key SEBI distinction: sharing YOUR perspective vs telling OTHERS what to do.

4. NO PRICE TARGETS - No forward-looking predictions of future stock prices.
   Show historical prices and say "This is where it has been" not "This is where it will go."
   Historical analysis with 90+ day old data is permitted.

5. NO LIVE PRICES - Use data that is at least 90 days old (SEBI Jan 2025 circular).

6. NO PERFORMANCE CLAIMS - Never say "If you had invested X years ago..."
   Say "This stock has grown X% over the past 5 years" (pure factual statement).

7. NO AFFILIATION WITH REGULATED ENTITIES - Do not appear to be endorsed by 
   SEBI, NSE, BSE, or any brokerage.

8. BALANCED PRESENTATION - When discussing a stock, include risks AND positives.

9. TRANSPARENCY - Any conflicts of interest must be disclosed upfront.
```

### 1.4 ASCI Guidelines for Financial Influencers

The Advertising Standards Council of India (ASCI) requires:
- If providing BFSI technical commentary, must hold verifiable qualifications
- For stock/investment content: Ideally SEBI registered
- Without registration: Only share generic, awareness-based content
- Qualifications/registration status must be displayed prominently

### 1.5 Risks of Non-Compliance

SEBI has taken aggressive action:
- **Avadhut Sathe case** (Dec 2025): ₹546 crore impounded, barred from markets
- **Asmita Patel case** (Mar 2025): ₹5.36 crore impounded
- Fines up to ₹1 lakh per violation
- Potential debarment from securities markets
- YouTube/Instagram can deplatform for regulatory violations

## 2.5 Channel Core Concept: Investing, Not Trading

### 2.5.1 Core Message
The channel's single, unwavering message: **Trading destroys wealth for 90%+ of people. Long-term investing is the only reliable path to wealth building.**

### 2.5.2 Why This Works
- **Truthful**: NSE data shows ~90% of active traders lose money (SEBI study, 2023)
- **Educational**: Teaches discipline, patience, fundamentals — real financial literacy
- **Broad appeal**: Relevant to EVERY Indian with a bank account, not just market enthusiasts
- **SEBI-safe**: Promoting long-term investing = textbook education. No conflict with regulations
- **Sustainable content**: Endless angles — company analysis, compounding stories, sector trends, behavioral finance
- **Brandable**: Clear mission = loyal audience. People follow a philosophy, not tips

### 2.5.3 Forbidden Content
- Trading strategies, intraday tips, F&O strategies, short-term calls
- "Get rich quick" framing, guaranteed returns, "secret formula"
- Live market commentary, real-time price discussion
- Promoting trading courses, paid groups, signals

### 2.5.4 Target Audience
- **Primary**: Salaried employees, small business owners, housewives — people who want to build wealth but don't know how
- **Tone**: "Spoon-feed information" — make complex concepts simple, relatable, action-oriented
- **Promise**: "Even a common man/woman can build wealth if disciplined enough to invest long term"

### 2.5.5 Channel Persona
- **Name**: Alex
- **Location**: Chennai, Tamil Nadu
- **Voice**: Alex's own voice (cloned for all three languages via XTTS v2 / Coqui TTS)
- **Style**: Fellow investor sharing HIS perspective, HIS analysis — not an expert telling people what to do
- **Framing**: "Here's what I look for in a stock" / "This is how I analyze a company" / "I would buy if..."
- **Authenticity**: Based in Chennai → naturally connects with Tamil-speaking audience, understands South Indian investor mindset
- **Ground rule**: Every time Alex says "I would buy/sell at X level", the NEXT sentence MUST be:
  > "But please do your own research before investing in this or any stock."
  This creates a **pattern interrupt** — the viewer hears the personal opinion, then immediately hears the disclaimer. No ambiguity.

### 2.5.6 "I Would..." Safe Harbor Protocol
Every statement of personal opinion MUST be immediately followed by a research-reminder:
```
❌ "I would buy Reliance if it drops to ₹2500." (stands alone - risky)
✅ "I would buy Reliance if it drops to ₹2500. But do your own research before 
    investing in this or any stock." (paired disclaimer - safe)
```
This mirrors the SEBI logic: you're sharing YOUR plan, not recommending an action. The follow-up reinforces:
- You're not telling anyone what to do
- Everyone must do their own research
- You're just sharing your personal strategy

## 2.6 Shorts Hooks Strategy

### 2.6.1 Problem with Single Hook
Using the same hook format ("Did you know X?" for every short) bores the audience and kills retention. Each short needs a fresh hook that grabs attention in the first 1-2 seconds.

### 2.6.2 Hook Categories (LLM Generates Randomly, No Repeat Within 7 Days)

| # | Category | Example |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Stat shock** | "90% of day traders quit within 2 years" |
| 2 | **Myth bust** | "The biggest lie about the stock market is..." |
| 3 | **Story open** | "3 years ago, a friend asked me where to invest ₹50,000..." |
| 4 | **Question** | "What if you could retire with ₹5 crore? Here's the math" |
| 5 | **Contrast** | "The difference between rich and broke investors is ONE habit" |
| 6 | **Challenge** | "Stop doing this ONE thing if you want to build wealth" |
| 7 | **What-if** | "What if I told you you don't need to pick stocks at all?" |
| 8 | **Relatable** | "We all know someone who lost money in F&O..." |
| 9 | **Visual** | "This chart shows why patience beats timing every time" |
| 10 | **Bold claim** | "Index funds beat 80% of professional investors. Here's why" |

### 2.6.3 Implementation
- LLM script prompt includes a `hook_category` parameter chosen randomly from 10+ categories
- The hook category determines the first 5 seconds of the script
- Prevent repeats: each generated short tracks its hook category, system ensures no same category within 7 days
- The hook must be ENTERTAINING + IMPACTFUL + TRUE all at once

## 3. YouTube Monetization Guidelines

### 3.1 YouTube Partner Program (YPP) Eligibility

| Requirement | Detail |
|---|---|
| Subscribers | 1,000 minimum |
| Watch Hours | 4,000 in last 12 months **OR** 10M Shorts views in 90 days |
| Location | Available in India |
| Policies | Must comply with Community Guidelines, monetization policies |
| Content | Must be original, authentic, not mass-produced |

### 3.2 Critical: YouTube's 2025-2026 Crackdown on AI/Automated Content

**This is the biggest risk to the project.** YouTube's July 2025 policy update:
- **"Inauthentic Content" policy**: Mass-produced, templated, AI-generated content with minimal human input is **ineligible for monetization**
- **What's targeted**: AI voice reading scripts over stock footage/slideshows
- **What's allowed**: AI as a *tool* with substantial human creativity, original commentary, clear educational purpose

**Implications for this project:**
- Cannot just have AI voice + stock footage = demonetized
- Need: Original script writing (not purely AI), unique visual narrative, educational value
- Must have "substantial human creativity" in the production pipeline
- The automated system should generate *drafts* that a human reviews/approves, not blindly publish

### 3.3 Advertiser-Friendly Guidelines for Financial Content

YouTube restricts monetization on:
- Content promoting dishonest behavior (pump-and-dump schemes, "get rich quick")
- Misleading financial claims
- Content causing "shocking content" or distress
- Content that could be considered enabling harm

**Green flags for financial content:**
- Educational, factual, balanced
- Clear disclaimers
- Cites sources
- No exaggerated claims ("double your money in 1 month")
- Professional presentation

### 3.4 Recommended Monetization Path

1. **Phase 1 (0-1000 subs)**: Focus on quality, build library
2. **Phase 2 (1000-10000)**: Apply for YPP, monetize long-form videos
3. **Phase 3 (10000+)**: YouTube Shorts monetization, memberships, Super Chat

### 5.2 Video Structure (Long-Form Deep-Dive Template)

```
[0:00-0:15] DISCLAIMER (mandated text on screen + voice): Educational purposes only, not SEBI registered
[0:15-1:00] HOOK: "Company X reported 20% profit growth. But should that excite you? 
            Let's look at what the numbers actually tell us..."
[1:00-2:00] QUICK OVERVIEW: What the company does, sector, market cap, brief history
[2:00-5:00] FUNDAMENTALS: Revenue, profit, margins, debt, cash flow trends (5 year chart)
[5:00-7:00] KEY METRICS: P/E, P/B, ROCE, ROE vs industry peers (comparison table)
[7:00-9:00] MANAGEMENT QUALITY: Promoter holding, track record, corporate governance 
[9:00-11:00] MOAT ANALYSIS: Competitive advantages, barriers to entry, market position
[11:00-13:00] GROWTH DRIVERS: What could drive future growth (new markets, products, etc.)
[13:00-15:00] RISKS: What could go wrong (regulatory, competition, macro, debt)
[15:00-17:00] MACRO CONTEXT: How does current economic environment affect this stock
[17:00-19:00] VALUATION: Where does it trade vs history? Overvalued/fair/undervalued? 
            (NO PRICE TARGET, just factual comparison to historical ranges)
[19:00-19:45] SUMMARY: Key takeaways viewer should research more
[19:45-20:00] DISCLAIMER REPEAT + CTA: Subscribe for educational content
```

## 6. Automation Pipeline Architecture

### 6.1 Data Sources

```
1. INDIAN STOCK MARKET DATA
   - DalalAI API (₹0-₹24,999/mo): Real-time, fundamentals, FII/DII, AI signals
   - IndianAPI.in (free tier available): Fundamentals, financials, stock data, news
   - Yahoo Finance (yfinance - free): Historical prices, basic financials
   - NSE/BSE official websites (free but rate-limited): Bhavcopy, corporate actions
   - TrueData (paid): Real-time data, option chain

2. NEWS & SENTIMENT
   - Google News RSS / News API: Company and market news
   - Moneycontrol / Economic Times / Livemint: Financial news scraping
   - SEBI website: Regulatory filings and announcements
   - NSE website: Corporate announcements

3. MACRO DATA
   - RBI website: Policy rates, inflation, GDP data
   - Investing.com: Global market data, commodities, forex
   - TradingEconomics: Macro indicators
```

### 6.2 Content Generation Pipeline

```
DATA LAYER
├── Stock Fundamentals Scraper (daily)
│   ├── Fetches: Revenue, Profit, Margins, Debt, Ratios
│   ├── Source: DalalAI API / IndianAPI / yfinance
│   └── Frequency: Daily (data refreshes overnight)
│
├── Market Data Aggregator (daily)
│   ├── Fetches: Nifty/Sensex levels, sector indices, FII/DII, advance-decline
│   ├── Source: NSE website, BSE website
│   └── Frequency: After market close (3:30 PM)
│
├── News Monitor (real-time / every 30 min)
│   ├── Fetches: Company announcements, market news, economic data
│   ├── Source: RSS feeds, Google News, SEBI filings
│   └── Frequency: Every 30 minutes during market hours
│
└── Scheduled Stock Selection
    ├── Picks: Predefined watchlist (Nifty 50 + Midcap 100 + selected smallcaps)
    └── Logic: News-driven (scandal/earnings) + calendar-driven (results season) + random rotation

ANALYSIS LAYER
├── Script Generator (LLM-based)
│   ├── Input: Raw data from Data Layer
│   ├── Process: Analyzes fundamentals, technicals, macro context, news
│   ├── Output: Video script following the template structure
│   └── Rule: Enforced NEUTRAL LANGUAGE (no advice, no targets, no recommendations)
│
├── Script Reviewer (automated check)
│   ├── Checks: SEBI compliance, neutral language, disclaimer presence
│   ├── Rejects: Scripts with buy/sell recommendations, price targets
│   └── Passes: Only compliant scripts to production
│
└── Human Review Queue
    ├── All scripts queued for quick human review before rendering
    └── If no review within 30 min, auto-approve (configurable)

MEDIA LAYER
├── Video Asset Fetcher
    ├── Source: Pexels (free stock footage), Pixabay (free stock footage)
    ├── Filter: HD quality, relevant to financial/stock market themes
    └── Fallback: Chart animations, infographics, text overlays
│
├── Audio Generator (TTS)
    ├── Provider: Edge TTS / Azure TTS / Gemini TTS
    ├── Voice: Natural-sounding, professional (English)
    └── Quality: High bitrate, natural pauses, proper emphasis
│
├── Subtitle Generator
    ├── Provider: Whisper (accurate) or Edge (fast)
    ├── Style: Clean, professional, at bottom-center
    └── Language: English
│
└── Video Renderer (MoneyPrinterTurbo)
    ├── Input: Script + assets + audio + subtitles
    ├── Process: Combine into final video with transitions, BGM, branding
    └── Output: 1080x1920 (vertical) for Shorts, 1920x1080 (horizontal) for long-form

PUBLISHING LAYER
├── YouTube Uploader (YouTube Data API v3)
    ├── Uploads long-form video to YouTube
    ├── Sets: Title, description (with disclaimer), tags, category (22 = Finance)
    ├── Schedules: Configurable publish time
    └── Privacy: Public
│
├── YouTube Shorts Uploader
    ├── Uploads vertical version to YouTube
    ├── Tagged as #Shorts
    └── Schedule: 2-4 per day
│
├── Instagram Uploader (Meta Graph API)
    ├── Uploads Reels (same vertical video as shorts)
    ├── Caption: Includes disclaimer + hashtags
    └── Schedule: Aligned with YouTube Shorts schedule
│
└── Cross-Platform Manager
    ├── Ensures consistent branding across platforms
    ├── Tracks upload status and errors
    └── Retries failed uploads
```
