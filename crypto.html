🚨 4 Harsh Realities of Indian Crypto Taxation

1. Zero Loss Offsetting Allowed

If you profit ₹2 Lakhs on Bitcoin and lose ₹2 Lakhs on Altcoins, your net real profit is ₹0, but the Income Tax Department requires you to pay ~₹62,400 in tax! Losses cannot be set off against other crypto gains.

2. Crypto-to-Crypto Swaps are Taxable

Swapping BTC for ETH or USDT triggers an immediate tax event. You are deemed to have sold BTC at fair market value and must pay 30% tax on gains even if you never cashed out to INR.

3. 1% TDS on Every Sell Transaction

Under Section 194S, Indian exchanges automatically deduct 1% TDS on total transfer value if annual transaction exceeds ₹50,000 (or ₹10,000 for specified persons), visible in your AIS/Form 26AS.

4. Foreign Exchanges (FIU-IND Compliance)

Offshore platforms (Binance, KuCoin) are now registered with FIU-IND (Financial Intelligence Unit). P2P bank transfers are heavily tracked by cyber cell and tax authorities.
