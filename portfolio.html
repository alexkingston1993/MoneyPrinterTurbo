📖 Step-by-Step Broker Statement Export & Import Guide

Exact click-paths to export your authentic stock statement from any Indian depository or broker.

🟦 Zerodha Console Holdings Export
1
Log in to Console
Open your web browser and navigate to console.zerodha.com. Sign in using your Zerodha Kite User ID, password, and 6-digit App Code / TOTP.
2
Open Portfolio > Holdings
In the top horizontal menu bar, click on the Portfolio tab, then select Holdings from the drop-down options.
3
Download CSV / XLSX
Look for the download icon (arrow pointing down) on the top-right corner above your holdings table. Click it and select Download CSV or Excel.
4
Import to Dalal Street Alpha
Click "Import Broker CSV" at the top of this page, drag & drop the downloaded Zerodha file, and your real holdings will aggregate in 1 second!
🟩 Groww Stocks Holdings Statement
1
Open Groww Web or App
Log in to groww.in on desktop or launch your Groww mobile application with your PIN / biometrics.
2
Navigate to Profile > Reports
Click on your Profile Picture (Avatar) in the top-right corner. In the dropdown, select Reports.
3
Export Holdings Report
Under the Stocks tab, locate Holdings Report. Click Download (it saves as a CSV/Excel file directly to your downloads folder).
4
Upload & Analyze
Drag and drop that statement into the Statement Import modal. All your equities, quantities, and buy prices will be auto-parsed.
🟧 DhanHQ Portfolio Export
1
Log in to Dhan Web
Go to web.dhan.co and scan the QR code using your Dhan mobile app or log in with credentials.
2
Go to Portfolio > Holdings
Click on Portfolio in the left/top sidebar, then click on the Holdings tab.
3
Click 'Export to CSV'
Click the table action icon labeled Export or Download CSV. (Alternatively, generate an API token via DhanHQ for real-time streaming).
4
Import & Cross-Audit
Select the Dhan CSV in Dalal Street Alpha to merge with Zerodha and Groww portfolios seamlessly into one unified view.
🟪 Upstox Pro Statement Export
1
Log in to Upstox Pro
Open pro.upstox.com or launch the Upstox app. Complete login with OTP/PIN.
2
Account > Reports
Click on your Account tab at the bottom/top navigation, then click on Reports & P&L.
3
Download Holdings Statement
Select Holdings Report, choose current valuation date, and click Export to CSV.
4
Import to Dalal Street Alpha
Upload your statement. Upstox tickers will be automatically mapped to NSE symbols and audited by the AI diagnostic engine.
🟥 Angel One Holdings Export
1
Log in to Angel One
Visit angelone.in or use the Angel One SuperApp. Authenticate with your client code and MPIN.
2
Open Portfolio Section
Click on Portfolio in the bottom/top navigation bar and click on All Holdings.
3
Download Statement
Tap the Download icon located near the total portfolio valuation to export the CSV/Excel sheet.
4
Import or Use SmartAPI
Drag and drop the statement here, or use the "Direct Broker API" tab to plug in your Angel One SmartAPI client code for live websocket pricing.
🏛️ CDSL / NSDL Consolidated Account Statement (CAS)
1
Visit Depository Portal
Go to the official depository e-CAS website at cdslindia.com or nsdl.co.in.
2
Generate e-CAS Statement
Click on e-CAS. Enter your 16-digit Demat Account BO ID (CDSL) or DP ID + Client ID (NSDL), PAN, and DOB. Verify the SMS OTP.
3
Unlock & Download Statement
Download your monthly e-CAS PDF or Excel sheet. (The standard default password is your 10-character PAN in uppercase, e.g. ABCDE1234F).
4
Import Cross-Depository Portfolio
Upload your CAS here to instantly audit all your demat shares across every single Indian broker in one single shot!

Can We Import a User's Portfolio by Simply Asking for Their 16-Digit Demat Account Number?

Regulatory Reality (SEBI & Depository Compliance): Under SEBI cybersecurity regulations and CDSL/NSDL Depository bylaws, NO website or third party can pull holdings simply by typing in a bare 16-digit Demat ID without authentic user consent. If any platform could inspect your holdings with just an account number, anyone with your demat ID could snoop on your private financial net worth.

The Legitimate Solution: SEBI-Approved Account Aggregator (AA) Framework: India’s Reserve Bank of India (RBI) and SEBI have established the regulated Account Aggregator (AA) ecosystem (such as Setu, Finvu, and OneMoney).

1. Enter Demat ID / Mobile
User inputs their 16-digit BO ID or registered Aadhaar mobile.
2. Direct Depository OTP
An official, cryptographically signed OTP is sent by CDSL/NSDL to the registered mobile.
3. Instant Encrypted Sync
Upon entering the OTP, live holdings are securely fetched with zero manual CSV exports!
