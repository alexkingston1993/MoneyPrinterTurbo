Affiliate & Brokerage Sharing Transparency Policy

Complete transparency regarding how Dalal Street Alpha monetizes its research portal, platform-administered fee incentives, and strict editorial separation.

🤝 Full Transparency: All Bonuses & Rebates are Provided Directly by Platforms

Dalal Street Alpha believes in complete transparency. We share informational partner and referral links on our website and in video descriptions. When you open an account or purchase a subscription through these links, the platform pays us a referral commission or brokerage share.

We do not process payments, collect funds, or give rebates from our pocket. All sign-up rewards, fee waivers, reward points, and trading discounts are funded, administered, and credited directly by the respective brokerage or trading platform.

1. Monetization Architecture: Three Distinct Streams

Our revenue model is organized into three transparent categories:

  • Indian Stock Brokers (SEBI Regulated): We share partner links to top technology-driven discount brokers (such as Dhan and Zerodha). These platforms provide ongoing brokerage sharing (typically 10% to 20% of net brokerage generated on executed trades, or up to 50%–70% for registered Authorized Persons under exchange bylaws).
  • Global & Crypto Derivatives Platforms: For global derivatives and cryptocurrency analysis (such as Delta Exchange India, Bybit, and Binance), exchanges share a percentage of net maker/taker trading fees. Delta Exchange India settles daily commissions directly in Indian Rupees (INR) with FIU-India registration.
  • Charting & Order-Flow SaaS Platforms: Software platforms like TradingView, GoCharting, and Bookmap pay recurring subscription commissions (15% to 30% of subscription fees). These are pure software platforms and do not execute securities orders.

2. Platform-Administered Referral Incentives

Where platforms provide affiliate settings (such as Binance or Bybit's referral partner dashboard), our links are configured to select the option where the exchange gives a fee discount directly to new sign-ups:

How Platform Discounts Work for You

When registering via our partner links on supported exchanges, the trading platform's automated engine automatically applies available fee rebates or sign-up bonuses directly to your trading account ledger. Dalal Street Alpha does not manage user accounts, hold funds, or disburse cash rebates.

3. Editorial Independence & Conflict of Interest Policy

To protect our quantitative integrity and comply with SEBI advertising guidelines:

  • No Paid Positive Coverage: No broker, exchange, or software company can pay us to alter technical analysis, modify market levels, or promote a biased point of view.
  • Zero Guaranteed Claims: We never promise that using a particular broker or indicator will guarantee profitable trades.
  • Separation of Content and Links: Market dispatches, options chain breakdowns, and macro commentary are generated purely from market data, completely independent of affiliate agreements.

4. Compliance with SEBI Finfluencer Circulars (2024–2026)

SEBI prohibits regulated entities from associating with unregistered individuals who provide buy/sell tips or promise returns. Dalal Street Alpha strictly complies with these guidelines by maintaining 100% educational commentary, displaying mandatory disclaimers, and funneling users transparently to our verified comparison hub rather than spamming unverified links.
