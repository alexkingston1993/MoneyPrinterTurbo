⚠️ Statutory Notice under SEBI (Investment Advisers) Regulations, 2013 & SEBI (Research Analysts) Regulations, 2014

Dalal Street Alpha, its creators, authors, algorithmic engines, and associated digital channels are NOT registered with the Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI) as an Investment Adviser (IA) or Research Analyst (RA). Under no circumstances should any analysis, data point, indicator, commentary, or video published on this domain be interpreted as financial advice, investment advisory, stock recommendations, or a solicitation to trade securities.

1. Purpose and Educational Scope

All content distributed by Dalal Street Alpha—including but not limited to written research reports, options chain open interest heatmaps, FII/DII institutional liquidity tracking, algorithmic radar models, YouTube videos, and automated social dispatches—is curated strictly for educational, mathematical, historical, and financial literacy purposes.

Our mission is to educate market participants on how financial markets function, how institutional volume behaves, how order flow and footprint charts are structured, and how technical indicators operate. We do not evaluate personal financial circumstances, risk tolerances, or individual investment objectives.

2. Strict Prohibition of Stock Tips, Calls, and Price Targets

In accordance with SEBI circulars governing unregistered financial content creators and market participants:

  • No Buy/Sell/Hold Calls: We never issue direct imperatives such as "Buy Stock X", "Sell Option Y", or "Take a position at level Z".
  • No Target Prices or Stop Losses: We do not publish forward-looking price targets, expected returns, or guarantee future market inflection zones.
  • No Portfolio Management: We do not manage funds, offer copy-trading, operate PMS pools, or accept capital for trading.
  • No Guaranteed Return Schemes: Any claim of guaranteed profits, risk-free returns, or "sure-shot" trading strategies is strictly contrary to our principles and securities laws.

3. The "Personal Perspective" Standard ("I Would" vs. "You Should")

To ensure transparent demarcation between prohibited investment advice and lawful educational commentary:

Permitted Framing: Personal Perspective & Historical Observation

Any discussion of price action or setup represents only the creator's personal analytical perspective or historical statistical observation (e.g., "Historically, when open interest clusters at 23,000, call writers tend to defend the strike" or "From my personal study, I would observe support near the 200 EMA"). This never represents advice directed at the reader or viewer.

4. Mandatory F&O Risk Disclosure (SEBI Study Findings)

Trading in the Indian Derivatives Segment (Futures & Options on NSE and BSE) carries severe financial risk:

SEBI Research Finding on Retail F&O Losses:

According to SEBI's comprehensive study on individual retail traders in equity derivatives:
• 9 out of 10 (93%) individual traders in equity F&O incurred net financial losses.
• Over and above net trading losses, loss-makers spent an additional 28% of net trading losses in transaction costs and brokerage.
• Those making net profits typically had high capital bases, institutional algorithms, or direct co-location execution infrastructure.

Every visitor, reader, and viewer is expressly advised to conduct their own independent research and due diligence before taking any financial decisions.
