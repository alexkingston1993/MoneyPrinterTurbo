📚 Understanding Unlisted Shares & Pre-IPO Investments in India

What is an Unlisted Share? Unlisted shares represent equity ownership in companies that are registered as private or unlisted public companies and are not yet formally traded on the NSE or BSE order books. Investing in unlisted equities allows institutions and high-net-worth investors to participate in profitable market monopolies (like NSE Ltd) or late-stage unicorns before public listing discovery.

1
Depository Crediting
Transferred directly into your existing CDSL or NSDL Demat account via off-market DIS / e-DIS transfer. Shares hold the same legal status as public equity.
2
SEBI 6-Month Lock-in Rule
Under SEBI (ICDR) regulations, all pre-IPO equity shares held by non-promoters are subject to a mandatory 6-month lock-in period from the official exchange listing date.
3
Taxation Treatment
Holding period > 24 months qualifies for Long-Term Capital Gains (LTCG) taxed at 12.5% (post-2024 budget). Holding < 24 months is taxed at applicable income tax slab rates.
⚠️ Regulatory Notice & Risk Disclosure: Unlisted equities carry liquidity risk as there is no formal exchange-governed order matching mechanism. Price discovery is determined bilaterally based on secondary market supply and demand. Past pre-IPO price appreciation is not a guarantee of listing day gains.
