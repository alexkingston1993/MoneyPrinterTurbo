Essential risk warnings regarding financial market speculation, leveraged derivatives, theta decay, and capital loss exposure for Indian and global markets.

🔴 HIGH CAPITAL RISK WARNING: YOU MAY LOSE 100% OF DEPOSITED FUNDS

Trading in securities, equities, exchange-traded funds, and specifically Futures and Options (F&O) involves a high level of risk and may not be suitable for all investors. The high degree of leverage that is often obtainable in derivatives trading can work against you as well as for you. You must carefully consider whether such trading is suitable for you in light of your financial condition, experience, and risk tolerance.

1. The SEBI Study on Retail Derivatives Losses

The Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI) published landmark empirical research analyzing retail participant performance in the Equity Derivatives segment:

  • Loss Frequency: 93% of individual retail traders in equity derivatives incurred net financial losses over the multi-year study period.
  • Average Loss Magnitude: The average loss per loss-making trader was approximately ₹1.25 Lakhs to ₹2.5 Lakhs, not including transaction costs.
  • Cost Burden: Active traders spent between 15% and 28% of their entire capital pool in non-refundable transaction fees, exchange turnover costs, STT, and broker commissions.
  • Institutional Dominance: Institutional algorithms, proprietary trading desks, and high-frequency market makers capture the vast majority of positive alpha.

2. Leverage Risk & Liquidation Mechanics

Derivatives allow market participants to control large notional values with small initial margin deposits. While leverage magnifies upside, it equally magnifies downside:

A minor unfavorable price movement in the underlying index (e.g., a 1% gap in Nifty 50) can result in a 20% to 100% loss of your invested option premium or trigger immediate margin calls on futures positions, requiring additional capital injection or forced liquidation at extreme market distress.

3. Time Decay (Theta) and Option Buyer Vulnerability

Options are wasting assets. Every passing second, options lose value due to Theta Decay (Time Decay):

The Zero-Value Expiration Reality

Out-of-The-Money (OTM) options contracts expire completely worthless (0.00 value) at weekly and monthly settlement if the underlying index fails to exceed the strike price. Buying cheap OTM options carries an extremely high probability of 100% capital loss.

4. Overnight Gap Risk & Black Swan Events

Indian stock markets operate between 9:15 AM and 3:30 PM IST. Major geopolitical events, US macroeconomic prints (CPI, FOMC rates), crude oil price shocks, and global market cascades occur while Indian exchanges are closed.

Holding naked overnight positions in options or index futures exposes participants to gap-up or gap-down openings that completely bypass stop-loss orders, leading to severe catastrophic slippage.

5. Technology, Connectivity, and Latency Hazards

Electronic trading systems are subject to internet outages, mobile application glitches, broker server downtime, API rate-limiting, and exchange connectivity freezes. Dalal Street Alpha assumes zero responsibility for trading executions, missed entries, delayed orders, or system outages on any third-party broker platform.

6. Absolute Capital Preservation Rule

Never trade with borrowed money, household emergency funds, retirement savings, or capital that you cannot afford to lose entirely. All quantitative research published on Dalal Street Alpha is designed to instill disciplined risk management and financial awareness. If you cannot tolerate rapid equity drawdowns, stay away from leveraged derivatives.
