    <h2>1. The Mathematics of Room Rent Caps</h2>
    <p>
        If your policy has a ₹5,00,000 sum insured with a 1% daily room rent cap (₹5,000/day), opting for a Deluxe Room at ₹10,000/day triggers a 50% proportionate deduction penalty. Under IRDAI guidelines, hospitals link doctor visits, surgeon fees, and nursing charges to the chosen room category. When the insurer scales down the room fee, they proportionately scale down all associated medical fees.
    </p>

    <h2>2. IRDAI Master Circular Protections & Solutions</h2>
    <p>
        To safeguard family savings against catastrophic out-of-pocket medical expenses, everyday policyholders should:
    </p>
    <ul>
        <li><strong>Opt for 'No Room Rent Capping' Riders:</strong> Ensure your base policy or super top-up explicitly removes room category limits.</li>
        <li><strong>Review Consumables Add-ons:</strong> Consumables (gloves, syringes, sanitizers, PPE) often make up 10–15% of modern hospital bills and are excluded without a specific rider.</li>
        <li><strong>Super Top-Up Deductibles:</strong> Combine a basic employer policy with a high-deductible personal super top-up to lock in affordable ₹25L+ lifetime coverage.</li>
    </ul>
