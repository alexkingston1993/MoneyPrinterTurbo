    <h2>1. Understanding Cumulative Volume Delta (CVD) Skew</h2>
    <p>
        CVD measures the net aggressive taker buy volume minus taker sell volume. When spot prices make higher highs while CVD diverges negatively, market makers are distributing spot holdings into breakout buyers while funding rates remain elevated, creating conditions ripe for a long squeeze.
    </p>

    <h2>2. Risk Management in High-Volatility Regimes</h2>
    <ul>
        <li><strong>Avoid High-Leverage Traps:</strong> Over 95% of liquidation cascades occur at leverage levels exceeding 10x due to tight liquidation boundaries.</li>
        <li><strong>Monitor Funding Rate Spikes:</strong> Extreme annualised funding (>40%) signals severe crowded positioning prone to mean-reversion.</li>
    </ul>
